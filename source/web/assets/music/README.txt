MUSIC SETUP — MAXIMUM 3 SONGS

1. Put MP3/OGG files and cover images here.
2. Open: web/assets/settings/config.js
3. Edit the music.tracks list.

Behavior:
- 1 song: repeats until loading finishes.
- 2 or 3 songs: next song starts automatically; after the last, it returns to the first.
- shuffle: true selects a random next song.

Keep audio files compressed to avoid slow loading.
